Is there a Notability alternative for Android that works offline?

Notability itself arrived on Android in 2026 (free to download, with a paid subscription for the full feature set, per 9to5Google). If you want something offline with no account and on-device AI, Penra (Android closed beta; Windows and iOS coming soon) offers audio-synced ink, colored folders, stickers and sticky notes, highlighter, pen on PDFs, photos and screenshots, plus handwriting-to-text and question answering over your notes.

What people usually miss from Notability, and what Penra has

  • Audio-synced ink: record while you write; tap any line later to hear what was said at that moment.
  • Folders with colors on top of tags.
  • Stickers, sticky notes, highlighter, pen colors and thickness.
  • Write on PDFs, photos and screenshots.
  • Added by Penra: handwriting and PDF to text, voice to text, search by meaning and Ask your notes, all on the device.

How Penra differs from Notability, Goodnotes and Samsung Notes

  • No account and no cloud: nothing is uploaded, and the AI runs on the device.
  • Much younger: Notability and Goodnotes are mature, polished apps with large user bases; Penra is a closed beta.
  • Platforms: Goodnotes lists Apple, Android, Windows and web; Samsung Notes is for Galaxy devices; Penra is Android only today.

When Penra is not the right choice

  • You need real-time collaboration or cloud sync between devices.
  • You need the app on iPad or Mac today.
  • Your device has less than about 6 GB of RAM (the on-device AI is memory hungry).

Questions

Is Penra free?

Every install gets all Pro features free for 7 days; then Pro is monthly, yearly or one-time lifetime via Google Play.

Does Penra have a version for iPad?

Not yet. iOS and Windows versions are in progress.

Penra is a free closed beta on Android. Windows and iOS are coming soon.
