Is there a note app that works offline without an account?

Yes. Penra is a note app for Android that needs no account and has no cloud. After a one-time download of its AI models, handwriting reading, voice to text, search by meaning and "ask your notes" all run on the device, including in airplane mode. Penra is in a closed beta on Android today; Windows and iOS versions are coming soon.

What "no account, no cloud" means in Penra

  • There is no sign-up and no Penra server: nothing to log in to and nothing that can receive your notes.
  • The internet is used only to download the AI models (from Hugging Face or Oracle Cloud storage), when you ask. After that the app works offline.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code.
  • Notes leave the device only if you share or export them.

Our own measurement on 24 October 2026: after the one-time model download, Android's network counters for Penra showed 0 bytes sent or received while we searched notes and asked the on-device AI a question. Details and how to repeat it: verify it yourself on your own device.

Other apps that work offline (and what differs)

  • Obsidian: free for personal use, notes are local Markdown files, and the app does not require an account (an account is only needed for paid add-ons such as Sync). It is text-first and has no built-in handwriting reading or on-device question answering.
  • Goodnotes: notebooks are stored on the device and can be used offline; its pricing page says sign-up is how you start, and its advanced AI features (for example AI for Meetings with cloud transcription) need paid plans.
  • Samsung Notes: works on Galaxy devices; syncing runs through Samsung Cloud on a Samsung account, and Samsung says Note Assist (Galaxy AI) needs a Samsung account and an internet connection.

Honest limits

  • Android only for now (closed beta). Windows and iOS are coming soon, not available yet.
  • The AI models are large: our measured first download was about 4 GB. Penra works best with 6 GB of RAM or more; the largest reading model needs about 8 GB.
  • Running AI on the device is slower than a cloud server: a full handwritten page takes about 30 to 60 seconds on a mid-range Android phone.
  • With no account and no cloud there is no account-based sync between devices.

Questions

Does Penra need an internet connection?

Only once, to download the AI models. After that it works fully offline, including in airplane mode.

Is the Penra beta free?

Every install gets all Pro features free for 7 days with no card. After that, Pro is monthly, yearly or a one-time lifetime purchase through Google Play.

Penra is a free closed beta on Android. Windows and iOS are coming soon.
