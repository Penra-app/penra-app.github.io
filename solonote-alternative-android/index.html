A SoloNote alternative for Android that also reads handwriting, PDFs and voice on the device

SoloNote by RolledAI (solonote.rolled.ai; not the unrelated one-note notepad also called Solonote) and Penra private notes for Android both need no account and run AI on the phone, once a model is on the device. That shared ground is not the difference. Penra private notes is built so photographed handwriting, multi-page PDFs and recordings become notes you can search on Android, with pens and a highlighter on photos and PDF pages. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. Stay with SoloNote if you want one free, no-subscription app on iPhone as well as Android, and a smaller model that ships with the app (about 1.3 GB on Android). Try Penra private notes if you want paper, PDFs and voice in one Android library, with core features free with no limits.

What SoloNote already does well

From SoloNote's own site and FAQ, checked 2026-11-11: it is free, with no subscription, no ads and no account, and it says it operates no servers for your content. It already ships on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (Android 10 or later, 64-bit). The AI model comes with the app, about 1.3 GB on Android and about 1.2 GB on iPhone since version 3.1, so there is nothing to fetch later for the main model. AI features on Android need 6 GB of RAM or more. The shared core includes notes, search by meaning, on-device answers from your notes (Chat on Android, Search & Ask on iPhone), Tidy, dictation, reminders, locked notes, trips and a home-screen widget, plus rich notes with photos, checklists and links. SoloNote "answers from what you wrote and shows the notes it used," and "when the answer isn't there, it says so instead of guessing." Airplane mode is their benchmark for notes, search, Search & Ask / Chat, writing help and reading a screenshot. Maps need a signal. If you want a free, no-account notebook on iPhone and Android, with a model that arrives with the app, SoloNote is built for that.

The difference is breadth, and a published way to check the network. Where the two tools differ, and where SoloNote is the better fit

  • Platforms. SoloNote is on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (Android 10 or later, 64-bit). Penra private notes is Android only; Windows and iOS are in progress, not available.
  • Price and download. SoloNote is free, with no subscription and no ads (vendor FAQ, 2026-11-11), and is the smaller download: about 1.3 GB, shipped with the app. For Penra private notes, core features are free with no limits; Pro is speaker separation, some additional AI models, and sync coming soon, with a 7-day Pro trial and no card. Pro prices are shown in the app. The first Penra download is about 4 GB; 6 GB of RAM or more is recommended. A handwritten page takes about 30 to 60 seconds on a mid-range phone: a limit, not a speed claim.
  • Android documents and photos. SoloNote's feature list says it can import a PDF, a Word file or a photo of a page and turn it into a note. The same day's FAQ and privacy policy are more specific for Android: files attached in Chat are read for that conversation only, "the text is never saved as a note," and discarded when you start a new chat. That is a vendor-page conflict; this page prefers the FAQ and privacy wording for Android. On iPhone, a long-press import becomes an ordinary note. Capture, Today and Spending are iPhone-only (vendor site, 2026-11-11).
  • Network, Ask, voice, locking and sync. SoloNote: maps need a signal; optional Android Chat web search (off by default) "sends your message to Tavily"; anonymous Firebase diagnostics unless turned off, never your notes, and some builds ship with diagnostics off. For Penra private notes: no Penra cloud or server. The internet is used only to download the AI models. The site says there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. SoloNote "answers from what you wrote and shows the notes it used," and says so when it cannot (vendor site, 2026-11-11); Penra's Ask your notes writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. SoloNote advertises dictation; meeting transcription and speaker separation are not stated. Penra private notes includes voice to text, free, with no usage limits; speaker separation is Pro. SoloNote can lock a note behind the device lock, and says locking "is not encryption." Penra's public facts do not describe a locked-notes feature. Neither product advertises a vendor cloud sync for notes. Penra sync is coming soon as a Pro feature and is not available yet.

How do SoloNote and Penra private notes for Android compare?

Both keep notes on the device with no account. SoloNote is free with no subscription, ships on iPhone and Android, and includes search by meaning, on-device answers from your notes, Tidy, dictation, reminders and trips; Capture, Today and Spending are iPhone-only. On Android, SoloNote's FAQ (checked 2026-11-11) says files attached in Chat are read for that conversation only and are not saved as a note; that conflicts with a more general feature card on the same site, so this page prefers the FAQ.
