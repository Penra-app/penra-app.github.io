How do I turn handwriting into text offline, without uploading it?

Use an app whose recognizer runs on the device. In Penra (Android, on Google Play) you photograph a page or pick a PDF, and an on-device AI model returns editable text in about 30 to 60 seconds per page on a mid-range phone. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed. Reading is tested most on English and Turkish pages; the app interface is in 15 languages.

How it works in Penra

  • Photograph a page (Penra flattens it and turns it upright) or import a PDF; multi-page documents are supported.
  • A vision-language model running on your phone reads full handwritten pages, forms with fields and numbers, lists and printed text.
  • You get editable text that is searchable by meaning and usable by "Ask your notes". Fix any word before saving.

Because the model runs locally, the page image never leaves the device. You can verify the no-upload claim yourself.

Other ways to do it offline

  • Samsung Notes converts S Pen handwriting to text through its clean-up handwriting tool (Samsung support page). That is for ink written on a Galaxy screen, not for photographs of paper.
  • Google ML Kit digital ink / text recognition can run on-device for developers. In an internal test on our own set of handwritten pages, ML Kit's on-device recognizer scored 31% against 89 to 91% for Penra's model; Turkish diacritic letters were ML Kit's weakest point. This is our own measurement, not an independent benchmark.
  • Most cloud OCR services upload your image to a server, which is the thing to avoid for private notes.

What to expect

  • Messy handwriting can still produce small misreads; the text stays editable.
  • Languages: tested most on English and Turkish pages. Other Latin-script languages work, with less testing.
  • Speed: about 30 to 60 seconds per page on a mid-range Android phone; faster devices are quicker. Many pages back to back can heat the phone.
  • Penra is on Google Play for Android today; Windows and iOS versions are coming soon.

Questions

Does Penra upload my handwriting photos?

No. The recognition model runs on the device. There is no Penra server to upload to.

Which languages can Penra read?

It reads Latin-script handwriting; it is tested most on English and Turkish. The app interface is available in 15 languages.

Penra is on Google Play for Android. Windows and iOS are coming soon.
