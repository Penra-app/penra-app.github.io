Verify it yourself

We say Penra collects nothing. You should not have to trust us. Your device counts every byte each app sends, and you can read it.

No account. No cloud. No internet.

Check on your own device (Android)

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Penra → Mobile data & Wi-Fi (on some devices: Data usage).
  2. Note the numbers, then use Penra for a day: scan pages, record voice, search, ask your notes.
  3. Look again. After the one-time model download, the numbers do not grow.
  4. Stronger test: turn on airplane mode. Everything in Penra keeps working.

Our measurement (24 October 2026, Penra from Google Play)

Android's own network statistics for Penra since install: one 2-hour window on 23 October with 4,073.5 MB received (the AI models) and 46.7 MB sent. The sent part is 815,433 packets of 57 bytes on average: the receipts (TCP acknowledgements) that every download sends back, no content. Since then: 0 bytes, in either direction. Live test on 24 October, 21:11-21:15: we opened a scanned note, searched, and asked the on-device AI “When is the train to Lyon?” (it answered “Nov 3 at 07:45” from the note). Penra's counters before and after, after forcing Android to refresh them: identical, 0 bytes.

Why nothing can be sent

  • No account, no sign-up, no Penra server.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising code: those libraries are removed from the app when it is built.
  • Internet is used only to download the AI models and word lists when you ask, from Hugging Face or Oracle Cloud storage.
  • After the models are downloaded, Penra works fully offline: you can even keep it in airplane mode.
