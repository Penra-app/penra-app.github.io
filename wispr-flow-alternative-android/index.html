A Wispr Flow alternative for Android that keeps your notes on the phone

Wispr Flow is cloud dictation: you speak, and it types clean text into the app you are using, on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. Its own pages say you create an account, transcription always happens in the cloud, and it does not work offline. Penra private notes for Android is a different kind of tool, a notes app: photograph a handwritten page, open a PDF or record a lecture, and the phone itself turns it into text you can search by meaning and ask, after a one-time model download. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. No Penra cloud or server. The internet is used only to download the AI models. Core features are free with no limits. Choose Wispr Flow if you want your voice typed into every app on several kinds of device. Choose Penra private notes if you want pages, PDFs and recordings in one set of notes on the phone, with no account, and you are fine with a new, Android-only app that does not type into other apps.

The official product is Wispr Flow by Wispr AI, Inc. (Play package com.wispr.flowapp). Wispr's own help center warns about fake Play listings called "Whisper Flow" (fake Android apps, 2026-11-19). It is also not OpenAI Whisper.

What Wispr Flow does well

From Wispr Flow's own pages and store listing, checked 2026-11-19:

  • Writes in the app you are already using: that is the product. On Android, "Flow works in most apps that use standard Android text fields," and it sits next to your keyboard rather than replacing it.
  • Cleans up as you speak: filler-word removal, punctuation from pauses and tone, self-corrections and numbered lists, in 100+ languages.
  • Several platforms, one account: Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android; with the same account, subscription and settings sync across devices.
  • Speed is their headline: the home page says "4x faster than typing" (220 wpm against 45 wpm on a keyboard). That is their claim; Penra has no comparable measurement.
  • Large store presence: the US Google Play listing showed 4.7 stars and 1M+ downloads (Google Play).
  • Compliance for teams: Wispr lists SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001, and HIPAA handling with a signed agreement on Growth and Enterprise. It says it does not sell your data.

If you want a dictation tool that types into every app, Wispr Flow is built for that. Penra private notes is not a replacement for that job.

What Penra private notes for Android does differently

No sign-up, no Penra server. Core features are free with no limits. Pro adds speaker separation, some additional AI models, and sync when it arrives (coming soon), with a 7-day trial and no card. The internet is used only when you ask to download the AI models; after that, these run on the phone:

  • Handwriting and PDFs to text: photograph a page or import a PDF and get editable text. See handwriting to text offline.
  • Voice to text: on-device transcription through the phone's microphone, free with no weekly word cap; speaker separation is a Pro feature. See the on-device Otter alternative.
  • Find by meaning: search for what a note is about, not only the exact words. See private second brain app.
  • Ask your notes, with the source shown: Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up.
  • Ink and audio: write on PDFs, photos and screenshots, and replay audio-synced ink.

Where Penra private notes is weaker (and where Wispr Flow is the better fit)

  • Other apps: Wispr Flow types into the app you already have open. Penra private notes does not.
  • Platforms: Wispr Flow runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. Penra private notes is Android-only; Windows and iOS are in progress, not available.
  • Meetings: Wispr Flow's Notetaker is listed on Mac, Windows and iPhone. Penra private notes cannot join Zoom or Meet calls; it records through the phone's microphone.
  • Maturity: Wispr Flow's US Play listing showed 1M+ downloads on 2026-11-19. Penra private notes is new on Google Play.
  • Languages: Wispr Flow lists 100+ dictation languages. Penra's handwriting reading is tested most on English and Turkish.
  • Phone requirements: Penra private notes needs about 4 GB of models once, 6 GB of RAM or more (about 8 GB for the largest reading model), and takes about 30 to 60 seconds per handwritten page on a mid-range phone. That is slower than a cloud server.
  • Sync: Wispr Flow is a signed-in service, so your account follows you across devices. Penra private notes has no account-based sync today; sync is coming soon as a Pro feature, and is not available yet.

Is there a Wispr Flow alternative for Android that works offline?

If what you want is private notes, Penra private notes for Android needs no account; after a one-time download of its AI models (about 4 GB) it reads photographed handwriting and PDFs, transcribes voice, searches by meaning and answers questions on the phone, including in airplane mode. Does Wispr Flow work offline?

No, according to its own Android page on 19 November 2026: "Does Flow work offline? No. Flow requires an internet connection to transcribe your speech using our fastest models." Penra private notes uses the internet only for the one-time model download; after that, reading pages, transcribing voice, searching and asking questions work in airplane mode.

Does Wispr Flow transcribe on the device?

No. Its privacy page on 19 November 2026 says "Transcription always happens in the cloud to provide the best speed and accuracy." Penra private notes transcribes on the device after the model download.

Do I need an account?

For Wispr Flow, yes: its getting-started guide says "Create an account or sign in" (checked 2026-11-19). Penra private notes needs no account and no sign-up.

Can Penra private notes replace Wispr Flow for typing into other apps?

No. Wispr Flow inserts dictated text into the app you already have open; on Android it works as a floating bubble next to your keyboard. Penra private notes is a notes app for Android and does not type into other apps. If that is the job, Wispr Flow is the better fit.
