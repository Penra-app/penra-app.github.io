Uteke, the Android notes app at mobile.uteke.app (not the separate signed-in product Uteke Cloud), and Penra private notes for Android both need no account and run on the phone after a one-time model download. That shared ground is not the difference. Penra private notes turns handwritten pages, PDFs and recordings into one set of notes you can search by meaning. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. Those core features are free with no limits. Stay with Uteke if you want a small, search-first Android notes tool and are happy inside the official Free caps, or willing to pay the published Pro price. Try Penra private notes if you also need paper and PDFs, uncapped voice to text, and Ask your notes showing lines from your notes.

What Uteke already does well

From Uteke's official site, checked 2026-11-11: no account, no cloud sync and no tracking. Its job, in its own words, is "Capture in seconds. Find by meaning." You can type or speak, add tags and collections, and search by meaning with a match score, fully offline after the model download. Airplane mode works after that one-time download. The search model is about 188 MB on first launch; voice dictation downloads its own small model the first time you use it. Backups are files you export and keep where you choose.

The official offer is "Free where it matters. Pay only for power." Free is $0, with 250 notes, 5 collections, 10 documents, full semantic search, 5 voice dictations a day, and no account. "Over-cap notes stay readable, only new creation pauses." The site footer also says Uteke is "Built on the open-source uteke core, Apache-2.0," linking github.com/codecoradev/uteke. This page has not verified whether the Play-listed Android client is that same repository.

If you want a small, search-first Android notes tool, with a published price and a much smaller download than a handwriting-reading model, Uteke is built for that.

What Penra private notes for Android adds

The difference is breadth, an uncapped free core, and a published way to check the network. Photograph a page or open a PDF and get editable text on the device, saved as a note. See handwriting to text offline. Voice to text and transcription are free, with no usage limits; speaker separation is Pro. See the on-device Otter alternative. Find by meaning. See private second brain app. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. You can also write with pens and a highlighter on photos, screenshots and PDF pages. No account, and no Penra cloud or server. The internet is used only to download the AI models. After a one-time model download of about 4 GB (our measurement on the verify page: 4,073.5 MB received in a 23 October 2026 window; 0 bytes during search and Ask your notes on 24 October 2026), it works offline, including in airplane mode. Core features are free with no limits.

Where the two tools differ, and where Uteke is the better fit

  • Job. Uteke is a search-first typed (and dictated) notes tool. The official site, checked 2026-11-11, does not advertise photographed handwriting-to-text, PDF reading as a core capture path, speaker-separated transcription, or an ask-your-notes chat. Penra private notes is built around pages, PDFs and recordings in one library. That is not a failure of Uteke at jobs it does not claim.
  • Free tier vs free core. Uteke Free is capped, per the official pricing block rechecked 2026-11-11. For Penra private notes, core features are free with no limits. Uteke Pro, on the official English site that day, is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year, with "2 months free" on the yearly price, "Prices in USD, billed through Google Play. Cancel anytime." Storefront prices may differ because billing goes through Google Play. Pro prices for Penra private notes are shown in the app.
  • Download, platforms and network. Uteke is the much smaller download: about 188 MB for the search model, plus a separate small dictation model the first time you use voice (vendor FAQ). Penra's first download is about 4 GB; 6 GB of RAM or more is recommended. A handwritten page takes about 30 to 60 seconds on a mid-range phone: a limit, not a speed claim. Uteke's RAM minimum is not stated on the official pages checked. Uteke is on Android today; the site says "Android beta, iOS on the way," with no date (2026-11-11). Penra private notes is also Android today; Windows and iOS are in progress, not available. Uteke says "Zero network calls after the one-time model download." That is Uteke's phrase; this page does not copy it for Penra private notes. For Penra: no Penra cloud or server. The internet is used only to download the AI models. The verify page reports 0 bytes after the download, and also recorded TCP acknowledgements during the download.
  • Sync, backup and open source. Uteke has no cloud sync; Pro adds scheduled auto backup and "Export everything, anytime," and backups are files you export. Penra private notes names user-triggered encrypted backups and a JSON export in the privacy policy; vendor sync is coming soon as a Pro feature and is not available yet. Uteke's footer points to an open-source core under Apache-2.0. Penra is not described on the Penra site as open source.

Questions

Is there a Uteke alternative for Android?

Yes, if what you want is a broader on-device library than typed and dictated notes with semantic search. Uteke (mobile.uteke.app, Play package dev.codecora.uteke; not the separate product Uteke Cloud) is already an Android notes tool with no account and no cloud sync. After a one-time download of its search model (about 188 MB) you can capture notes and find them by meaning, including in airplane mode. Its official Free plan, checked on 2026-11-11, caps you at 250 notes, 5 collections, 10 documents and 5 voice dictations a day. Penra private notes for Android also needs no account and has no Penra server. After a one-time model download of about 4 GB it can read photographed handwriting and PDFs, turn voice into text with no usage limits, and search notes by meaning, all on the device. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. Core features are free with no limits. Sync is not available yet (coming soon as a Pro feature). If you want a small, search-first app and are happy inside those caps, Uteke remains the simpler fit.

How do Uteke and Penra private notes for Android compare?

Both keep notes on the phone with no account, after a one-time model download. Uteke focuses on fast capture and search by meaning. Its official site, checked 2026-11-11, does not advertise photographed handwriting-to-text, PDF reading as a core path, or an ask-your-notes chat. Pro on that site is $4.99 a month or $49.99 a year (USD, billed through Google Play; storefront prices may differ). Penra private notes is built so handwritten pages, PDFs and recordings become notes you can search. Voice to text is free, with no usage limits; speaker separation is Pro, with a 7-day Pro trial and no card. Penra's first download is much larger (about 4 GB) and it recommends 6 GB of RAM or more. After the models are downloaded you can check with airplane mode or Android's data counters (our measurement is on the verify page) that nothing further is sent. Pro prices for Penra private notes are shown in the app.
