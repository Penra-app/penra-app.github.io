Penra vs Notability, Goodnotes, Samsung Notes, Obsidian, Otter and XNote

Among the apps in this table, Penra has no account and no Penra cloud or server, and runs its AI (handwriting reading, voice to text, search by meaning, and Ask your notes: Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up.) on the device. It is also the youngest: new on Google Play for Android (from 2026-11-12), with Windows and iOS coming soon. Pick Notability or Goodnotes for mature handwriting apps across more platforms, Samsung Notes if you own a Galaxy with an S Pen, Obsidian for free local Markdown, Otter for cloud meeting transcripts with team sharing, and XNote if you want a smart pen that captures real paper. SoloNote is the stronger fit if you also need iPhone, or a free app with no subscription and a smaller built-in model; see the SoloNote alternative for Android. Uteke is the stronger fit if you want a small, search-first install and are fine with the official Free caps; see the Uteke alternative for Android.

Where Penra is weaker

  • Android only for now, and new: on Google Play from 2026-11-12.
  • SoloNote already ships on iPhone as well as Android, is free with no subscription, and has a smaller built-in model; Penra private notes is the stronger fit for photographed handwriting and PDFs saved as Android notes, and for voice to text with no usage limits. More: SoloNote alternative for Android.
  • Uteke is the stronger fit if you want a small, search-first install and are fine with the official Free caps. Penra private notes is the stronger fit for handwriting and PDF reading, uncapped voice to text, and Ask your notes. More: Uteke alternative for Android.
  • Wispr Flow is the stronger fit if you want your voice typed into the app you already have open, on Mac, Windows, iPhone or Android; Penra private notes does not type into other apps. More: Wispr Flow alternative for Android.
  • No sync or collaboration yet. Sync is coming soon as a Pro feature; it is not available today.
  • AI on the device needs memory (6 GB or more recommended) and is slower than a server: about 30 to 60 seconds for a handwritten page on a mid-range phone.
  • Handwriting reading is tested most on English and Turkish.

Questions

Which note app needs no account?

Penra private notes, SoloNote and Uteke need no account. Obsidian also does not require an account for its core app. Goodnotes, Otter and Samsung cloud sync use accounts.

Which note app runs AI on the device?

Penra runs handwriting reading, voice to text, meaning search and Ask your notes (Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up.) on the device. SoloNote and Uteke also run their AI on the device. Otter processes audio in the cloud, and Goodnotes' advanced AI features and Samsung Note Assist rely on online services.

Penra is on Google Play for Android from 2026-11-12, and its core features are free with no usage limits.
