Penra vs Notability, Goodnotes, Samsung Notes, Obsidian and Otter

Penra is the only one of these six that has no account and no cloud and runs its AI (handwriting reading, voice to text, search by meaning, questions over notes) on the device. It is also the youngest: new on Android (Google Play), with Windows and iOS coming soon. Pick Notability or Goodnotes for mature handwriting apps across more platforms, Samsung Notes if you own a Galaxy with an S Pen, Obsidian for free local Markdown, and Otter for cloud meeting transcripts with team sharing.

Where Penra is weaker

  • Android only for now, and newly released.
  • No account-based sync or collaboration: with no cloud there is nothing to sync through.
  • AI on the device needs memory (6 GB or more recommended) and is slower than a server: about 30 to 60 seconds for a handwritten page on a mid-range phone.
  • Handwriting reading is tested most on English and Turkish.

Questions

Which note app needs no account?

Penra needs no account and has no cloud. Obsidian also does not require an account for its core app. Goodnotes, Otter and Samsung cloud sync use accounts.

Which note app runs AI on the device?

Penra runs handwriting reading, voice to text, meaning search and question answering on the device. Otter processes audio in the cloud, and Goodnotes' advanced AI features and Samsung Note Assist rely on online services.

Penra is on Google Play for Android.
