What is a private second brain app, and how does Penra work?

A "second brain" is a place that keeps what you capture and helps you find and use it later. Penra is a private one: it captures handwriting, PDFs, photos and voice as text, finds notes by meaning, and answers questions from your own notes, with every AI step running on your Android device and no account or cloud. Penra is in a closed beta on Android today; Windows and iOS versions are coming soon.

The parts

  • Capture: handwriting photos, multi-page PDFs, voice recordings, text, ink on top of photos and PDFs.
  • Find by meaning: an on-device embedding model matches ideas, so "the meeting about money" finds a note that says "Budget for Q4". In our internal test, paraphrase search recall at 5 results rose from 6% for keyword search to 88% (our own small test, not an independent benchmark).
  • Ask your notes: ask a question in your own words and get the line that answers it with the note it came from.
  • Connect: related notes and links are suggested; folders, tags, templates and to-do boards organise them.
  • Protect: app lock, and hiding private details before sharing.

Why "private" is a stronger claim here

  • Many "private" apps mean "encrypted on our servers". Penra has no account and no server; the AI models run on the device.
  • You can check the claim yourself with Android's own network counters: verify it yourself on your own device.

Other second-brain tools

  • Obsidian: free for personal use and local-first, with notes as Markdown files you link yourself; it has no built-in handwriting reading.
  • Cloud tools (Notion, Evernote and similar) sync across devices through a server and an account, which is a trade-off, not a flaw.
