An XNote alternative for Android that keeps the AI on your phone

XNote is a smart pen and notebook (XNote Write, $199) whose app turns your pages into text, answers and tasks; its AI runs in the cloud, needs an internet connection and a sign-in, and its free plan has limits on AI chat and recording minutes. Penra private notes for Android needs no pen and no account: photograph a handwritten page or open a PDF, record a lecture, and the phone itself reads and transcribes after a one-time model download. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. Its core features are free with no usage limits; Pro adds only small extras. Choose XNote if you want to write in real ink and have every page captured automatically, on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. Choose Penra private notes if you want the AI to work offline with nothing leaving the phone, and you are fine with a new, Android-only app.

Not to be confused with XNotes (com.xnotes), an unrelated open-source Android handwriting app.

What XNote does well

From XNote's own pages:

  • Real pen, real paper: you write in ink on XNote notebooks or on pages you print from the app on a laser printer, and each page appears in the app as you write. Without a phone nearby, the pen stores up to 1,000 pages for later import.
  • Unlimited handwriting-to-text on the free plan, with editable results and 100+ languages to choose from.
  • Ask and summarize: ask a question and get the answer from your own pages; AI chat is included, with usage limits that paid plans raise.
  • Meetings: record in the room with the phone app, or let the meeting recorder join Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams from your calendar and email you a summary.
  • Tasks and integrations: send tasks and events to tools such as Todoist and your calendar.
  • Everywhere: iPhone, iPad, Android and a web app that can pair the pen in Chrome, Edge or Opera.

If you love writing on paper and want a mature, multi-platform cloud service, XNote is built for that; Penra private notes only launched on Google Play on 2026-11-12.

What Penra private notes for Android does differently

No sign-up, no Penra server, no extra hardware. Core features are free with no usage limits, not a trial; Pro adds small extras: speaker separation, some additional AI models, and sync when it arrives. The internet is used only when you ask to download the AI models; after that, these run on the phone:

  • Handwriting and PDFs to text: photograph a page or import a PDF and get editable text. See handwriting to text offline.
  • Voice to text: on-device transcription through the phone's microphone, free with no minute quota; speaker separation is a Pro feature. See the on-device Otter alternative.
  • Find by meaning: search for what a note is about, not only the exact words. See private second brain app.
  • Ask your notes, with the source shown: Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up. XNote also answers questions from your pages, but in the cloud.
  • Ink and audio: write on PDFs, photos and screenshots, and replay audio-synced ink.

To check that nothing is sent after the download, see the verify page.

Where Penra private notes is weaker (and where XNote is the better fit)

  • Paper capture: XNote's pen captures pages as you write. With Penra private notes you photograph a page or import a PDF after the fact.
  • Platforms: XNote runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web. Penra private notes is Android-only (on Google Play from 2026-11-12); Windows and iOS are in progress, not available.
  • Sync: XNote keeps your notes in your account across devices. Penra private notes has no account-based sync today; sync is coming soon as a Pro feature, but it is not available yet.
  • Meetings: XNote's recorder can join Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls. Penra private notes cannot join calls; it records through the phone's microphone.
  • Languages: XNote lists 100+ handwriting languages. Penra's handwriting reading is tested most on English and Turkish.
  • Phone requirements: XNote's app runs on Android 7.1 or later, with the heavy work in the cloud. Penra private notes needs about 4 GB of models once, 6 GB of RAM or more (about 8 GB for the largest reading model), and takes about 30 to 60 seconds per handwritten page on a mid-range phone.

Cost and privacy, in plain terms

Cost. XNote Write costs $199, and XNote says no subscription is needed: handwriting-to-text is unlimited on the free plan. Its limits sit elsewhere: AI chat has usage limits, and recording gets 150 audio minutes a month; when they run out, transcription pauses until the minutes renew or you buy a plan or extra minutes. Penra private notes needs no hardware, and its core features (handwriting and PDF reading, voice to text, search by meaning and ask-your-notes) are free with no usage limits; Pro adds speaker separation, some additional AI models and, when it arrives, sync.

Privacy. XNote says it does not use customer data to train general-purpose AI models, but your pages and recordings are processed in the cloud, partly by named third-party AI providers. Penra private notes has no account and no server to send them to; after the model download you can test that with airplane mode or Android's data counters.

Try Penra private notes for Android if you want handwriting reading, transcription and Ask your notes on the phone itself. Ask in your own words. Questions

Is there an XNote alternative for Android that runs AI on the phone?

Yes. XNote pairs a $199 smart pen with an app whose handwriting-to-text and AI run in the cloud and need an internet connection and a sign-in. Penra private notes for Android needs no pen and no account; after a one-time download of its AI models (about 4 GB) it reads photographed handwriting and PDFs, transcribes voice, searches by meaning. Ask in your own words. Penra writes a short answer from your notes and shows which note it came from; if it's not in your notes, it won't make it up, all on the device. Its core features are free with no usage limits. It is Android-only, needs 6 GB of RAM or more, and sync is not available yet (coming soon as a Pro feature).

Is XNote free to use?

The pen and notebook cost $199, and XNote says no subscription is needed: the free plan includes unlimited handwriting-to-text, search and AI chat with your notes. AI chat has usage limits, and recording gets 150 audio minutes a month; for more, XNote sells Pro ($19 a month or $99 a year) and Unlimited ($249 a year) plans and extra minutes, with store prices that may differ. Penra private notes needs no hardware, and its core features, including on-device transcription and ask-your-notes, are free with no usage limits; Pro adds speaker separation, some additional AI models and, when it arrives, sync.

Does XNote work offline?

Partly. The pen writes on paper and stores up to 1,000 pages without a phone, but XNote says handwriting-to-text and AI need an internet connection, and the first pairing needs internet too. Penra private notes uses the internet only for the one-time model download; after that, reading pages, transcribing voice, searching and asking questions work in airplane mode. It has been owner-tested on a Galaxy A34, Galaxy S24 FE and realme GT 2 Pro.

Penra private notes is on Google Play for Android from 2026-11-12: core features are free with no usage limits, and Pro extras come with a 7-day free trial, no card.
