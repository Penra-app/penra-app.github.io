Is there an Otter alternative that transcribes on the device instead of the cloud?

Penra (Android, on Google Play) records and transcribes voice entirely on the device: text appears while you speak, there is no account, and the audio is never uploaded. Otter is a cloud service: its privacy policy names Amazon Web Services for compute and storage and lists training its AI on de-identified audio and transcripts as a purpose. The trade-off is that Penra does not join online meetings as a bot.

What Penra does with voice

  • Record a lecture, meeting or idea; text appears while you speak.
  • Telling speakers apart is a Pro feature (Pro is free for the first 7 days).
  • Tap a line of your handwritten notes to hear the audio from that moment (audio-synced ink).
  • Ask your notes questions over the transcripts, and find them by meaning.

Otter versus Penra, plainly

  • Where it runs: Otter processes audio on its servers (privacy policy: Amazon Web Services, United States). Penra processes it on your phone.
  • Account: Otter is an account-based cloud service. Penra needs no account.
  • Meetings: Otter is built for online and calendar-linked meetings, with sharing and team features. Penra records through the device microphone and has no cloud sharing.
  • Price: third-party sources list a free Otter plan of 300 minutes a month with per-conversation limits; confirm on otter.ai/pricing. Penra transcribes on your own device, so there is no per-minute cloud processing.

Limits to know

  • Accuracy depends on language, microphone and noise. Try your language during the 7-day trial before relying on it.
  • On-device transcription uses battery and memory; it is not instant on older phones.
  • Penra is on Google Play for Android today; Windows and iOS versions are coming soon.

Questions

Does Penra send recordings anywhere?

No. Recording and transcription happen on the device and there is no Penra server.

Can Penra join Zoom or Google Meet calls?

No. It has no cloud, so it cannot join calls as a bot. It records through the device microphone.
